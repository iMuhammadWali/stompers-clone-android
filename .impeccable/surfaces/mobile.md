# Android account and friends flow

Mode: Operate. Primary target: src/app/(tabs)/friends.tsx. Related: authentication,
requests, add-friend, and profile screens. Audience: people using a phone to connect
with existing friends through exact usernames. All sample people are explicitly demo data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A walking club's people directory. Friends and invitations lead the app;
no fabricated step metrics or dashboard furniture.

OWN-WORLD: Cool light gray canvas, white surfaces, charcoal ink, orange contained actions, peach
selection surfaces, and mint, peach, gray, and yellow avatar containers, flat divided lists, themed Material 3 inputs and buttons. System
sans through a consistent type scale; one small geometric footprint mark.

STORY: Enter an account or explore labeled demo people; find an existing friend,
handle a request, or invite a username with an explicit success state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact brand row with Add friend, a large Your people heading,
an incoming-request action when relevant, a list search, and actual friend rows.
Bottom navigation has Friends, Requests, and You. No hero or decorative stats.

FORM: Phone-first directory with Material navigation and sequential account flow.
seed: none (unavailable). Context engine refused its unverifiable binary download;
code-led direction selected from the existing project brief, without a blocking picker.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
